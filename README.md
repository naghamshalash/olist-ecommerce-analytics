
# Olist E-Commerce Analytics

**Customer Satisfaction & Delivery Performance Analysis**

**Project Type:** Academic Semester Project  
**University:** Bar-Ilan University  
**Degree:** Industrial and Information Systems Engineering  
**Tools:** Excel, Power Query, Power BI, Statistical Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes the Brazilian Olist e-commerce dataset to investigate how delivery performance and other operational factors are associated with customer satisfaction. The goal was to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights and an interactive dashboard supporting data-driven decisions.

## Project Objectives
- Identify factors associated with customer review scores.
- Evaluate the relationship between delivery delays and customer satisfaction.
- Explore delivery trends, product categories, and geographic differences.
- Identify operational risk areas and propose actionable recommendations.

## Methodology
1. **Data Preparation:** Cleaned, merged, and transformed multiple datasets using Power Query.
2. **Data Modeling:** Built order-level and order-category-level analytical tables to support accurate aggregation.
3. **Exploratory Data Analysis:** Used Excel Pivot Tables, descriptive statistics, and correlation analysis to identify patterns.
4. **Statistical Testing:** Applied Welch's t-tests, ANOVA, Kruskal-Wallis, and Chi-square tests to assess relationships and statistical significance.
5. **Business Intelligence:** Developed a four-page interactive Power BI dashboard using DAX measures and dynamic filters.

## Key Findings
- Late deliveries received an average customer rating of **2.27/5**, compared with **4.29/5** for orders delivered on time or early.
- Customer ratings dropped noticeably when delivery delays reached **4 days or more**.
- Delivery performance was strongly associated with customer satisfaction, while price and freight-related factors showed much weaker relationships.
- Not all negative reviews were linked to late deliveries, suggesting additional opportunities to improve customer experience.

## Business Value
The analysis highlights delivery reliability as an important area for operational improvement. The dashboard helps identify patterns across time, geographic regions, delivery performance, and product categories to support management decisions.
